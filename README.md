# marvelousco
Hi, I'm Jordan 👋

Founder of Marvelous Co — I help small operators turn how they already work into systems that scale, using practical AI and a method I call the Marvelous Process Engine (MPE).

I spent years in logistics fixing the unglamorous bottlenecks — dock time, overtime, throughput — before building Marvelous Co to do that work for owner-led businesses. Everything here is real tooling from that work, not demos.


What I build


🛠️ Marvelous Process Engine (MPE) — a repeatable method for mapping a business process and rebuilding it as an AI-assisted system.
⚔️ Excalibur — a leadership assessment that maps how someone leads to one of seven archetypes.
📖 Fable Format — a story-driven content method for turning a client's real work into narrative ads.


How I think about AI

Not as a replacement for people — as a way to give small teams leverage they couldn't afford before. Honest claims, real metrics, no hype.
