# Excalibur ⚔️

**A leadership assessment that maps how you actually lead to one of seven archetypes.**
Built by [Marvelous Co](https://marvelousco.net).

---

## What it is

Excalibur is a scenario-based leadership assessment. Instead of asking people to rate themselves on abstract traits, it puts them in realistic decision moments and reads their leadership pattern from the choices they make.

The result is a single, memorable archetype — drawn from the knights of the Round Table — plus a full profile of how that style shows up under pressure.

## Who it's for

Founders, operators, and team leads who want a clear, non-generic read on their leadership style — and a shared language for talking about it with the people they lead.

## The seven archetypes

| Archetype | The leader who… |
|-----------|-----------------|
| **Arthur** | unites people around a shared vision |
| **Lancelot** | leads from the front and sets the standard |
| **Gawain** | holds the line on loyalty and principle |
| **Percival** | learns relentlessly and grows into the role |
| **Tristan** | leads through craft, intuition, and feel |
| **Bedivere** | steadies the team and gets things done quietly |
| **Merlin** | sees around corners and guides from insight |

## How it works

1. **Self-perception opener** — how you think you lead, captured before the scenarios.
2. **14 scenario questions** — realistic leadership situations, one at a time.
3. **4 open-ended questions** — to add nuance the multiple-choice can't.
4. **Scoring** — your responses map to the seven archetypes.
5. **Profile report** — your dominant archetype, your blend, and where your style helps and where it can trip you up.

> The scoring methodology is proprietary to Marvelous Co and not published here. This repo describes the assessment and what it produces.
